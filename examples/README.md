# ESPHome + Home Assistant Examples

## Hardware Requirements
- Compatible iRobot® Roomba®
- Buck Converter (Step down the Roomba's Vbat to 3.3V or 5V as needed by your uC)
- Transistor or Signal Level Buffer (if your uC is not 5V tolerant; ESP32 is)
- An ESPHome Capable Device
  - Seeed Studio's XIAO ESP32C3
  - ESP32-C3 Super Mini
